# Aurelstone style notes
Learned from purchased Quark library previews on 2026-10-26. Use these to generate original images. Do not publish the source photos.

## Warm minimal / 现代简约
- Built-in shelving with mixed open and closed bays
- Neutral grey-beige carcass, pale stone floor
- One saturated chair as the only colour
- Faceted glass pendants, textured stone band above the shelves
- Fluted glass screen at the edge
- Not empty. Styled, but not cluttered.

## Quiet luxury / 轻奢
- Crystal or glass linear chandelier, white ceiling with soft curves
- Taupe vertical-grain wall panels, white marble island
- One round hammered-metal art piece
- Green or deep blue chair as accent
- Grey marble floor, black recessed band
- Gloss on stone, matte on timber.

## New Chinese / 新中式
- Pale oak, white stone, black bowls
- Vertical timber screen, not heavy lattice
- White sculptural object, moss, ink-dark ceramics
- Daylight through slats
- Quiet, not temple-like.

## Cream / 奶油风
- Folder name is cream, first image was a grey tiled bathroom. Do not trust folder names alone.
- For cream: warm beige, soft edges, pale stone, warm light, no cold grey tile.

## Rules
- Generate original rooms. Do not copy a source photo.
